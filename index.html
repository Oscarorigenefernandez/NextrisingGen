Who we are

Hope grows when
we show up.

Where a child starts should never determine how far they can go.

NextGen Rising is a youth-led community initiative built on a simple belief: every child deserves opportunity, encouragement, and the support of a caring community.

We bring people together to make practical support and positive opportunities more accessible to children and families. Our work begins locally, with a commitment to listen, learn, and grow responsibly.

A message from our founder

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

Meet Oscar.

OONEXTGEN RISING

Oscar Origene Founder, NextGen Rising

“I created NextGen Rising because I believe every child deserves the chance to grow, learn, and build a brighter future—no matter their circumstances.”

I wanted to create something that could make a positive difference for children and families who may not always have access to the support and opportunities they need. I also wanted to show that making a difference doesn’t have to begin with a big budget. It can begin with people who care, showing up, working together, and taking one meaningful step at a time.

That belief is why we’re starting with Helping Hands: a community-first effort focused on practical support, educational resources, and bringing people together. My hope is to grow NextGen Rising from a local effort in New Jersey into a wider movement that helps communities thrive.

With purpose,
Oscar Origene
