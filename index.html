Who we are

It starts when
we show up.

A child’s circumstances shouldn’t decide what they’re able to achieve.

NextGen Rising started with a simple idea: every child deserves to feel supported and have a fair chance to succeed. I wanted to do something practical to help make that happen.

For now, that means focusing on our local community, listening to what families actually need, and finding people and organizations who want to help. We’re learning as we go and want to build this the right way.

A message from our founder

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

Meet Oscar.

Oscar Origene Founder, NextGen Rising

“I started NextGen Rising because I wanted to do something that could help kids and families in a real way. I believe every child deserves support and a chance to build a good future.”

I’ve seen how much a little support can matter. Not every family has the same resources, and I didn’t want to just talk about that—I wanted to try to help. I’m starting this without a big budget or a huge team. I’m starting with an idea, a willingness to work, and the hope that other people will want to be part of it.

That’s why the first project is Helping Hands. I want to find useful ways to support children and families, starting with things like school supplies and local service projects. I know this will take time, but I’d love to see it grow from a small New Jersey effort into something that can help more communities in the future.

With purpose,
Oscar Origene
